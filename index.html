ICS Global Account Team: Email Signature

Two clicks: copy it here, paste it into Outlook.

Step 1. Copy the signature

If the button does nothing, click inside the box below, press Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on Mac), then Ctrl+C (Cmd+C).

Step 2. Paste it into Outlook

New Outlook (Windows or Mac)
  1. Click the gear icon (Settings), then Accounts, then Signatures.
  2. Click + New signature and name it "ICS Team".
  3. Click inside the editing box and paste (Ctrl+V or Cmd+V).
  4. Under "Select default signatures", choose this signature for new messages and replies. Click Save.
Classic Outlook (Windows)
  1. File, then Options, then Mail, then Signatures.
  2. Click New, name it "ICS Team", click OK.
  3. Click in the big editing box and paste (Ctrl+V).
  4. Set it as default for new messages and replies on the right. Click OK.
Outlook on the web
  1. Settings (gear), Accounts, Signatures.
  2. New signature, name it, paste into the box.
  3. Tick the defaults for new messages and replies. Save.

Then send yourself a test email to check it looks right.
